# Privacy Policy

Stoic YouTube does not collect, store, sell, or transmit personal data.

The extension runs locally in your browser and only modifies the YouTube web interface to hide Shorts and blank the homepage feed.

## Data Collection

Stoic YouTube does not collect:

- Personal information
- Browsing history
- Search queries
- Video watch history
- Account information
- Location data
- Analytics data

## Data Sharing

Stoic YouTube does not share data with any third party.

## Permissions

Stoic YouTube requests access to:

- `https://www.youtube.com/*`
- `https://m.youtube.com/*`

These permissions are used only to hide YouTube Shorts and homepage recommendation surfaces on YouTube pages.
